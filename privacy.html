Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

LucidShield ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

LucidShield collects and processes the following data, all of which stays on your device unless you explicitly choose otherwise:

  • Health & Sleep Data — heart rate, motion, and sleep stage data accessed through Apple HealthKit, used solely to provide sleep tracking and REM detection features.
  • Audio Recordings — voice memos recorded through the Dream Journal feature, stored locally on your device.
  • App Preferences — settings such as cue timing, notification preferences, and theme choices, stored locally.

2. How We Use Your Data

All data is processed on-device to power the app's features, including sleep analysis, REM detection, nightmare rescue, lucid dream induction cues, and sleep history charts. We do not transmit your health data, audio recordings, or personal information to any external server.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal data to third parties. No analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or tracking tools are included in the app.

4. HealthKit

LucidShield uses Apple HealthKit to read heart rate and sleep data. This data is used exclusively within the app to provide sleep insights. In compliance with Apple's HealthKit guidelines, we do not store HealthKit data in iCloud, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with third parties.

5. Subscriptions

LucidShield Pro subscriptions are managed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information. Subscription status is verified on-device using Apple's StoreKit framework.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all data at any time by deleting the app. We do not retain any data after the app is removed.

7. Children's Privacy

LucidShield is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at support@oneiros.app.
